AI engines cite the brands the internet already trusts.

Large language models are trained and grounded on a finite set of trusted sources - major publications, Wikipedia, Reddit, expert-led blogs, structured directories. We engineer your presence across those exact source types through digital PR, original research, founder bylines, and Wikipedia-grade entity work.
