AI search engines don't crawl the web the way Google does — and they don't rank the way Google does either. They pattern-match against trusted sources, structured data, and citation density. We restructure your site, schema, and authority signals so ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity parse your brand correctly, cite you confidently, and recommend you when it matters. Citation Authority

AI engines cite the brands the internet already trusts.

Large language models are trained and grounded on a finite set of trusted sources - major publications, Wikipedia, Reddit, expert-led blogs, structured directories. We engineer your presence across those exact source types through digital PR, original research, founder bylines, and Wikipedia-grade entity work. The result: when someone asks an AI "who's the best [your category]," your name is one of the few it has the data to confidently surface.
