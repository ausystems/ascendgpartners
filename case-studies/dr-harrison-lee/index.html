How Ascend Built Dr. Harrison Lee's Media Authority Across Beauty, Lifestyle, and Mainstream Press

01

The Problem

His quiet precision wasn't getting the airtime it deserved.

Dr. Harrison Lee is a triple board-certified facial plastic surgeon with practices in Beverly Hills, Manhattan, and Dubai, known for the kind of meticulous, natural-looking results that earn celebrity referrals. But in a category crowded with louder, flashier marketers, his quiet precision wasn't getting the airtime it deserved.

His main goal was clear: build a media presence that matched the caliber of his work and reached the high-end patients searching for a surgeon they could trust.

Before partnering with us, Dr. Lee's expertise was an open secret among Hollywood insiders, but his digital footprint didn't reflect his standing in the field, leaving authority on the table that patients, press, and partners couldn't easily find.

02  ·  Our Strategy

Precision placements paired with category-spanning coverage.

Our game plan was all about precision placements paired with category-spanning coverage.

Starting from the foundation, we identified the outlets where Dr. Lee's patient base actually lives — beauty, lifestyle, celebrity, and mainstream news. We tested various angles and quickly discovered that pairing his technical expertise with the celebrity-adjacent storytelling readers expect from these outlets resonated far stronger than clinical positioning alone.

Armed with this insight, we crafted a campaign architecture that layered specialty features with high-authority list placements.

This wasn't just chasing logos — it was a deliberate sequencing of placements designed to dominate his name on Google, build trust with prospective patients, and reinforce his standing among industry peers.

Every step was aligned with the goal of not just getting Dr. Lee featured, but turning that coverage into measurable patient inquiries and category authority.

Anchored the campaign with feature stories in USA Today, Glamour, and The Source covering Dr. Lee's approach to facial rejuvenation and his celebrity transformation work.

04

Our Results

01

Took over the first page of Google for Dr. Lee's name with mainstream and lifestyle outlets, turning patient research into pre-qualified consultation requests.

02

Strengthened the practice's positioning across Beverly Hills, NYC, and Dubai by giving each location a press footprint that travels with the patient base.

03

Unlocked stronger conversion on consultation inquiries, with prospective patients arriving already familiar with Dr. Lee's reputation through the publications they trust.

05

Proven Success

A media footprint that does the credibility work before the consultation begins.

Our strategy turned Dr. Lee's quiet expertise into a visible authority asset.

By honing in on what truly matters — placement quality, sequencing, and aligning each feature with how high-end patients actually research surgeons — we didn't just get him featured; we built a media footprint that does the credibility work before the consultation begins.

The compounding effect of stacked tier-1 features across beauty, lifestyle, and mainstream press, combined with consistent "Top Doctor" recognition, showcases our commitment to not only landing coverage but making it work for the practice.

This approach benefited Dr. Lee in the short term as well as set a winning foundation for continued growth, proving that with the right strategy, even the most competitive medical specialties can be navigated successfully.

06

Key Takeaways

01

Tier-1 mainstream and lifestyle features significantly outperformed industry-only press, demonstrating the power of meeting patients where they actually read.

02

By laddering coverage across beauty, celebrity, and national news outlets, we made sure Dr. Lee's name appeared everywhere prospective patients look during their research, maximizing the impact of every placement.

03

Successfully turning press into stronger consultation conversion and category authority illustrates the effectiveness of treating PR as a trust-building system rather than a list of logos.
