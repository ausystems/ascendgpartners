David Feldman & BKFC

See how Ascend Growth Partners secured Forbes, USA Today, E! News, and Yahoo Sports coverage for David Feldman and Bare Knuckle Fighting Championship.

01

Client Overview

He had the story, the vision, and the results. What he needed was the media presence to match.

David Feldman is the founder and president of Bare Knuckle Fighting Championship (BKFC), the first sanctioned bare-knuckle fighting organization in modern history. Launched in 2018 with just four events, BKFC has grown into one of the most talked-about combat sports promotions in the world, producing 40+ events annually and attracting top talent from boxing and MMA alike.

Feldman came from a lineage deeply rooted in Philadelphia boxing, mentored by his father Marty Feldman, a celebrated trainer and promoter. He had the story, the vision, and the results. What he needed was the media presence to match.

That is where Ascend Growth Partners came in.

02

The Challenge

BKFC was growing fast, but David Feldman's personal brand was not keeping pace with the organization's momentum. Combat sports is a crowded, loud space. Without a deliberate media strategy, even legitimate achievements get buried.

Feldman needed coverage that would do three things:

01

Establish him as the definitive authority in bare-knuckle fighting.

02

Reach audiences far beyond the core combat sports fanbase.

03

Create a media footprint that compounded over time rather than fading after a single news cycle.

The goal was not press for its own sake. It was authority that opened doors, attracted investors, and put BKFC in rooms it had not been in before.

03  ·  Our Strategy

Meet the audience where they are, not just where combat sports fans already look.

Our press and PR strategy for David Feldman was built around a single idea: meet the audience where they are, not just where combat sports fans already look.

We identified that Feldman's story had crossover appeal well beyond sports media. His journey from Philadelphia boxing roots to founding an entirely new sanctioned combat sports category, growing it from near bankruptcy to a $400 million valuation, was a business story as much as a sports story. That framing unlocked a tier of publications that would have ignored a standard sports pitch.

We sequenced placements deliberately, starting with anchor features that established credibility, then building outward into broader entrepreneurship and entertainment publications to expand his reach into new audiences.

Every placement was chosen to compound the last, building a search presence, a media footprint, and a public narrative that made David Feldman synonymous with the future of combat sports.

04

Placements Secured

Forbes

We secured a Forbes feature on how Feldman built BKFC from near bankruptcy to a $400 million valuation. One of the most credible business publications in the world, this placement positioned Feldman as a serious entrepreneur and operator, not just a promoter.

01

USA Today

We featured Feldman and BKFC as the new powerhouse in combat sports, reaching a mainstream American audience that extended well beyond the core fighting fanbase.

02

E! News

We secured two separate features, including "Can David Feldman Become the New King of Combat Sports?" and a placement on the "Top 10 Entrepreneurs to Follow in 2024" list. These placements put Feldman in front of entertainment and lifestyle audiences and created a pattern of recognition across multiple years.

03

Yahoo Sports

We secured coverage revealing BKFC's valuation, driving significant traffic from sports audiences actively following the growth of alternative combat sports.

04
05

What This Coverage Accomplished

01

Dominated search results for David Feldman's name.

Tier-1 outlets now own the first page of Google for searches on Feldman, meaning every investor, partner, broadcaster, or potential signee who researches him encounters a wall of credibility before any conversation begins.

02

Expanded BKFC's audience beyond combat sports.

By securing placements in Forbes, E! News, and USA Today, we brought BKFC in front of business readers, entertainment audiences, and mainstream sports fans who had never encountered bare-knuckle fighting before.

03

Reinforced the $400M valuation story.

The Forbes feature gave Feldman a credible, high-authority source to point to when discussing BKFC's business trajectory with investors, broadcasters, and prospective partners. That single placement does ongoing business development work.

04

Created a multi-year pattern of recognition.

Two separate E! News features across different years, combined with Forbes and USA Today coverage, signal sustained relevance rather than a single moment. That pattern is what separates a press mention from a media authority.

05

Turned coverage into a sales and partnership asset.

As covered in our post on what a growth marketing agency actually does, earned media at this level does not just generate awareness. It shortens sales cycles, raises the floor on inbound deal quality, and builds trust before the first conversation happens.

06

The Bigger Picture

A nine-figure valuation in under a decade.

BKFC is one of the most compelling growth stories in sports. David Feldman built something from scratch in a space that did not exist, grew it through sheer conviction and operational grit, and reached a nine-figure valuation in under a decade.

Our job was to make sure the world knew that story, told it in the right publications, and sequenced it in a way that compounded authority rather than scattered it.

That is the Ascend approach to PR for ambitious businesses. Not chasing logos. Building footprints that do the work long after the article goes live.
